Questions

What does ATS-friendly mean?

Most companies run resumes through an applicant tracking system before a person reads them. Our templates use one reading order, real selectable text, standard section names and common fonts, so the system extracts your details correctly.

Will the AI make things up?

No. It's instructed never to invent employers, tools or numbers. When a metric would help, it leaves a highlighted placeholder such as [X%] for you to replace with the real figure.

Can I import my existing resume?

Yes. Upload a PDF, Word or text file and it becomes editable sections. Scanned images can't be read yet; export a text-based PDF instead.

How does tailoring work?

Pick a job from your tracker or paste a job post. You'll see which keywords you match and which are missing, then get suggested rewrites for your summary, bullets and skills. Accept the ones you agree with, or save them all as a separate copy.

What's the difference between Pro and Elite?

Both include every template, unlimited resumes and full version history. Pro covers an active search with 100 reviews and 100 tailorings a month. Elite makes reviews and tailorings unlimited, for heavy appliers and career coaches.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes, from your account page. You keep your plan until the end of the billing period, and your resumes stay yours on the free plan.
