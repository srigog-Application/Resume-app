FAQ

Questions, answered

What does “ATS-friendly” mean?

Most companies run resumes through an applicant tracking system before a human sees them. Our templates use a single reading order, real selectable text, standard section names and common fonts, so parsers extract your details correctly.

Will the AI make things up?

No. The AI is instructed never to invent employers, tools or numbers. When a metric would help, it inserts a highlighted placeholder like [X%] for you to fill in with the real figure.

How does job tailoring work?

Paste a job description. You'll instantly see which keywords you match and which are missing. Then AI suggests a targeted summary and reordered, reworded bullets. You choose what to apply, and can save the result as a new version or a separate copy.

Can I keep different versions for different jobs?

Yes. Save named snapshots of any resume, restore them anytime, or duplicate a resume into a tailored copy for a specific application.

What's the difference between Pro and Elite?

Both unlock every template, unlimited resumes and version history. Pro covers an active search with 100 analyses and 100 tailorings a month. Elite makes analyses and tailorings unlimited for heavy appliers and career coaches.

Can I import my existing resume?

Yes. Upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT file and we'll turn it into editable sections, then run an analysis so you know what to fix first.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription from your account page. You keep Pro until the end of the billing period, and your resumes are always yours.
